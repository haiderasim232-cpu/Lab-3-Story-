The Escape

Everyday routine gives way to adventure. A flight carries someone away from their desk and toward a peaceful beach holiday.

What changes when the order changes?

The airplane can represent an exciting departure or a journey home. The desk can be the routine someone leaves behind or the reality they return to. The images stay the same, but their place in the story changes their meaning.
